# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

Koma is a personal app that syncs your images and files between your devices through your own Google Drive. It has no server of its own.

## What Koma accesses

Koma asks for one Google permission: `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`. This lets Koma see and edit only the files and folders it creates. It cannot read the rest of your Drive.

## What Koma stores

- Your images and files are stored in your Google Drive and in a local copy on your device.
- Your Google sign-in tokens are stored privately on your device.

## What Koma shares

Nothing. Koma does not send your files, tokens, or any usage data to the developer or to any third party. The only network traffic goes to Google, to sign in and to sync with your Drive.

## Your control

- Click **Disconnect** in Koma to delete the saved tokens from your device.
- Revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Delete Koma's folder in your Drive to remove the synced files.
